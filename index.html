Our School

Education With Purpose, Character and Excellence

The Royal Garden Academy (ROGA) is committed to providing quality basic education while nurturing the intellectual, moral, spiritual and practical development of every learner.

Our approach combines academic learning with religious and moral discipline and practical skills to help children and young people prepare for a productive future.

We believe every learner deserves an environment that encourages curiosity, responsibility, creativity and a lifelong love of learning.

Our Vision

Raising a New Generation of Quality and Excellent Leaders Worldwide.

Our Commitment

To equip learners with knowledge, sound character, practical abilities and the confidence to contribute positively to society.

What Guides Us

Our Three Core Pillars

Our educational philosophy is built on three foundations that work together to develop well-rounded learners.

01

Academic Excellence

We promote quality teaching, meaningful learning, intellectual growth and strong educational foundations that prepare learners for future opportunities.

02

Religious and Moral Discipline

We encourage integrity, respect, responsibility, faith and positive values that guide learners in school and in their communities.

03

Practical Skills

We value creativity, useful life skills, problem-solving and the development of abilities that help learners apply their knowledge in everyday life.

The ROGA Difference

Building a Strong Foundation for Life

We aim to provide a supportive learning environment that helps every learner grow academically and personally.

Quality Education

Our educational program follows the prescribed Liberian Ministry of Education curriculum for the relevant school levels, supported by appropriate learning resources.

Dedicated Teachers

We value trained and qualified teachers who take a genuine interest in learners' progress and individual development.

Royal Writing

Our signature Royal Writing program emphasizes handwriting development, presentation and the cultivation of careful written work.

A Caring Community

We seek to build positive relationships among learners, teachers, families and the wider community in support of children's educational development.
